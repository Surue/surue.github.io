# Overview

Hell'Eluja is a VR game built around asymmetric multiplayer. One player uses a VR headset and explores the dungeon while another uses a tablet to spawn monsters and try to stop them.

## My work

During a one-month collaboration with OniroForge, I helped the team finish the game before launch by:

- building a new level with artists
- implementing monsters specific to that level
- reworking systems so they supported the new content correctly
